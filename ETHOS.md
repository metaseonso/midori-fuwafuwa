## The studio

A production studio based out of a Dreamland, shared by Seonso and Grumpy Carrot.

She owns the muse. He owns the genius, as the Greeks say.

## What we make things for

We make it for ourselves too.

We want to be an entity that makes things beautifully and thoughtfully, and gets appreciated
over time.

## What we believe

They say not to get lost in your fantasies.

But we know you're a secret genius with a method to your madness.

So we say: **don't get lost in your fantasies — let's find ourselves through them.**

## How we work

Everything runs on a rhythm — months, seasons, harvests. It's the same rhythm on every level of
the studio.

We'd rather be reached out to than track everything. There's enough of that already.
