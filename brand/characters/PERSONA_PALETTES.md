# Artist persona palettes

**These are not studio design tokens.** They belong here, not in `brand/DESIGN_SYSTEM.md`.

Seonso and Grumpy Carrot are the artist personas who established Midori Fuwafuwa and work in
the studio. **Each has their own distinct style, deliberately.** These palettes describe *them*
— use them when drawing or writing the personas, not when building the production site.

The site's design language comes from one place only: `brand/logo/midori_fuwafuwa_logo_official.png`.
See `brand/DESIGN_SYSTEM.md` §0.

## Seonso

Source: `seonso/seonso_ref_sheet.jpeg` — the sheet's own 12-swatch palette block, sampled
directly with PIL.

| | Col 1 | Col 2 | Col 3 | Col 4 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Row 1 | `#201F20` | `#4C4444` | `#97877E` | `#DDC1AC` |
| Row 2 | `#6E5548` | `#A68772` | `#D2A67C` | `#ECD8C8` |
| Row 3 | `#6A5649` | `#AF9178` | `#C8B9BA` | `#8A6850` |

Reading of the set:
- `#201F20` — his black. The anchor of his whole look.
- `#4C4444`, `#97877E` — warm greys stepping up from it.
- `#DDC1AC`, `#ECD8C8` — parchment and cream.
- `#6E5548`, `#6A5649`, `#A68772`, `#AF9178` — the brown/taupe body of the palette.
- `#D2A67C` — his gold. The accent that reads as ornament on the dark robes.
- `#C8B9BA` — a cool lavender-grey. The fox-spirit tone; the only cool colour he owns.
- `#8A6850` — base brown of the spiral seal swatch, sampled off the gold mark.

**Character of the palette:** dark, warm, low-saturation, with one gold accent and one cool
ghost-tone. Calm and weighty. Almost no pure hue anywhere.

Reading of the set:
- `#FDB1B6`, `#FBCFC5` — her pinks, the signature.
- `#FD9C78`, `#DE8F5C` — coral into carrot orange.
- `#E4A897`, `#E9B998` — dusty rose and tan, the mid-tones.
- `#443333` — her near-black. Warm, not neutral (sampled off the swatch's highlight).
- `#C3BF7C`, `#737C40` — carrot-top greens, light and deep.

**Character of the palette:** bright, high-chroma, warm-forward. Pink and orange dominate, with
one hard dark and a green pair drawn straight from a carrot. Energetic where Seonso's is calm.

## Why they differ

The two personas are different artists with different styles, and the palettes show it plainly:

|  | Seonso | Grumpy Carrot |
|---|---|---|
| Value range | Dark-anchored | Light-anchored |
| Saturation | Low throughout | High throughout |
| Temperature | Warm neutrals, one cool ghost-tone | Warm, almost no cool |
| Accent | A single gold | Pink *and* orange *and* green |
| Feeling | Calm, weighted, still | Bright, busy, moving |

Neither is the studio's palette. The studio's is the logo's: cream, mint, blush, ink, sage, wood.
