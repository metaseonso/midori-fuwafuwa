## ⏱️ Staleness convention

Research notes carry a `verified:` date and a `staleness:` rating in their frontmatter, plus a
warning banner at the top. **HIGH** means platform terms, fees or policies that change every year —
re-verify before acting. **MEDIUM** means slower-moving facts. Anything with a date in it should be
treated as a snapshot, not a standing fact.
