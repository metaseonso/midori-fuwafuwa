# How people arrive — the channel answer

> ⚠️ **Verified August 2026.** Platform user numbers, algorithms and monetisation terms change
> monthly. Re-verify anything load-bearing before committing time to a channel.

Full research: [[../../research/case-study-04-how-people-arrive|Case Study 04]].

## The finding that reframes everything

> **Every social acquisition window in the research has closed — and most closings can be dated.**

Across a dozen verified cases only **three growth archetypes** appear:

1. **Messaging-keyboard distribution** — Pusheen, Chibird, Capoo. *Closed.* Pusheen's Facebook slot
   was **granted**, not earned — Facebook approached the creator. Capoo entered LINE when there were
   ~100k sticker sets; there are now ~10M.
2. **Feed arbitrage** — Catana Chetwynd, Sarah's Scribbles, Chiikawa, Puuung. *Closed.*
3. **The B2B rights market** — Hilda, Labubu, Pilkey, Klassen, Jeffers. **Still open.**

> **The only archetype still open in 2026 is the one that never involved building an audience at
> all.**

### The near-controlled experiment

**Catana Chetwynd:** 0 → 2.8M followers in **27 months** (~104k/month). Then 2.8M → 3.1M over the
next **90 months** (~3k/month) — *while becoming more famous.* Same artist, same work, better
known. A **35× collapse** in growth rate that had nothing to do with her.

She also **started on Reddit**, not Instagram.

### The base rates, from primary filings

- **WEBTOON FY2025 10-K:** 27 million creators, *"the vast majority… amateur"* with no revenue
  share. MAU **−7.1%**. Net loss **$346.1M**.
- **Patreon:** 341,764 paid creators sharing $25.8M/month — a **~$75.50 mean**, median far lower.
- **Picture books:** every top-selling title is backlist from **1947–1992**. School-visit fee surveys
  price an ALA award at **+$480/day** and a NYT bestseller at **+$708/day** — **there is no follower
  coefficient.** Nobody pays for reach in this industry.

**The strategic conclusion:** *build the small thing you control, stay legible to the big thing you
cannot.*

## Do this

| Channel | Cadence | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| **A seasonal letter** (Buttondown, no tracking) | Per season — the studio's own rhythm | The only **owned** audience. Off-site subscribe page, so the site keeps its zero-form footing |
| **A shop that exists *before* traffic** | — | The B2B/direct path is the one still open. Puuung proves it: **$126,644 / 1,828 backers (2015)** and **$100,238 / 1,717 backers (2026)** — after Naver dismantled its creator economy in between |
| **Reddit** | Twice a year | The one **evidence-backed cold-start venue.** Where Catana actually started |
| **Instagram** | Monthly | **Capture and industry legibility, explicitly not growth.** Art directors look; the algorithm will not help you |
| **RSS → Bluesky** | Automatic | Free. Costs nothing to pipe |
| **Pinterest** | Seasonal | A cheap bet. ⚠️ See the warning below |

## Don't do this

**Webtoon / Tapas** · **Substack** · **TikTok / YouTube** · **Discord** · **any daily cadence.**

A daily cadence is incompatible with two artists who want to remain artists — and the research found
no evidence it would work anyway.

## ⚠️ Platform-specific warnings

**Pinterest — actively hazardous for a hand-drawn studio.** Its auto-tagger has been falsely
labelling **decade-old human artwork as "AI modified"**, with no removal path except appeals. And
*art* is one of the categories users can opt out of seeing. Real human art, suppressed as AI. US &
Canada growth has also stalled at **+4%** while headline growth comes from low-monetisation regions.

**Tumblr — best long-tail mechanics, disqualifying conflict.** Reblog chains and tag archives give
posts genuine multi-year life, and the demographic fit (18–24, majority female) is excellent. **But
Automattic has been selling Tumblr user content to Midjourney and OpenAI for AI training since
February 2024, opt-out by default.** A direct conflict for an art studio.

**Bluesky — the kidlit industry is genuinely there, but the platform is shrinking.** ~46M registered
accounts, **~3M daily** — roughly 6–7% show up on a given day, and mobile MAU is **down 52% from
peak**. The kidlit community (Kate Messner's list of 1,600+ accounts, art directors, agents) was
documented in January 2025, *before* that decline. Chronological feed means low-frequency posting
gets near-zero reach. Free via RSS; do not invest more than that.

**Substack — the only place you own the audience**, and verified illustrated publications run
18k–112k subscribers. But it is a direct-to-reader business tool, not an industry watering hole, and
its 2026 direction is toward being a social network. **Buttondown is recommended instead** — same
owned list, no algorithmic pivot, no Nazi-moderation controversy attached to the brand.

## Can the Forest be the publication?

**The grove can be the publication. The Forest cannot be the publisher.** Three independent reasons:

1. **Wrong audience.** Auto-generated process content interests other makers, not readers.
2. **A measured trust penalty** — **31% vs 7%** — against content perceived as AI-authored.
3. **Google's scaled-content-abuse policy** names this exact shape.

**The fix is small:** a **~2 hour editorial pass per season**, which conveniently doubles as the
[[../legal/ai-made-art-and-copyright|human-authorship pass]] that makes the writing protectable.

That is four times a year. It fits the studio's rhythm and it is the difference between a published
thing and a generated feed.
