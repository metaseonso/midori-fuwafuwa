# Sticker marketplaces

> ⚠️ **Verified August 2026.** Platform rules, fees and splits change. Kakao has **never officially
> published** its revenue split — every figure circulating is reported, not confirmed.

Relevant because the studio's IP is chibi characters, which is precisely what these markets sell.

## The finding that matters most

> **Nobody who succeeded got found by browsing. Not one.**

Every documented winner was escalated by a **specific mechanism**, never by uploading a good set
and being discovered:

| Case | The actual mechanism |
|---|---|
| **망그러진 곰** | A **Kakao curation slot**. Distributed free nationwide for 90 days → on conversion to paid, **#1 in every age bracket day one, #1 overall for 14 consecutive days.** Now on Crocs, bank cards, convenience-store collectibles, school textbooks. |
| **Bugcat Capoo** (Taiwan) | LINE stickers → won **LINE's Webtoon competition** → serialisation → licensing. The *adjacent vertical* was the escalator, not the sticker shop. |
| **오버액션토끼** | **Cross-platform arbitrage** — a LINE-native Japanese IP imported into an under-served Kakao market. |
| **최고심** | **Built an external audience first** (Instagram, Grafolio). Marketplaces were the payment rail, not the discovery. |
| **잔망루피** | A meme the **rights-holder owned** and rode. |

**So: stickers are a payment rail plus a curation lottery. Design for the curation slot, and bring
your own audience.**

## ⚠️ The warning that lands directly on this studio

**잔망루피.** A fan created the meme. The **rights holder** released the emoticon. The rights
holder went on to get Bvlgari, a Jeju Air aircraft livery, a government campaign, and a One Piece
collaboration.

**The person who made the meme got nothing.**

> **Own your characters before a channel amplifies them.**

That is what [[../legal/trademark-launch-checklist]] is for, and this is why it matters rather than
being paperwork.

*(Second warning: JYP Entertainment copied illustrator 최고심's designs for merchandise without
agreement, and only settled after public backlash. Small studios get copied by large ones.)*

## The three markets

### ✅ LINE Creators Market — the open door

**The one the studio can join today, from anywhere.**

| | |
|---|---|
| **Eligibility** | *"Anyone with a LINE account can register."* No residency, no business registration |
| **Review** | **Hours to ~2 days**, with **itemised rejection reasons** |
| **Submissions** | Up to **30 review requests per day** |
| **Revenue** | **50% of net** after store fees (≈35% of gross). Full 50% on LINE STORE web sales |
| **Payout** | **PayPal** (Premier or Business — *not* Personal) outside Japan/Thailand. Minimum **¥1,000** |
| **Tax** | **20.42%** Japanese withholding for non-residents; reducible via tax-treaty form |
| **Formats** | Static sets of 8/16/24/32/40; animated 8 or 16. PNG only, ≤1MB each |

**Why it is first:** iteration speed. Hours-to-days review with stated reasons is the only feedback
loop compatible with two people learning what lands. It is also the channel that produced Capoo and
오버액션토끼.

### ⚠️ Kakao Emoticon Studio — the prize, not the entry

| | |
|---|---|
| **Shelf rate** | ~**1,500 proposals/week → ~20 released ≈ 1.3%** *(2022 figure — the hardest number available, now dated)* |
| **Timeline** | **2–6 months** to shelf |
| **Rejection reasons** | **Never given.** Rejected creators filed suit in 2020 over this |
| **Revenue split** | **Never officially published.** Reported: 30% store / 40% Kakao / **30% creator** |
| **Non-Korean settlement** | ⚠️ **Undocumented publicly.** Must be confirmed by direct inquiry |

**Enter it with a character that already has an audience.** Kakao's own "Creator of the Year"
programme selects on sales, send counts, **IP operation status**, and user response — it explicitly
rewards IP you are already building elsewhere.

**Scale check:** 850,000 individual emoticons released cumulatively. Only **2,885 have ever crossed
₩100M gross in 14 years** — apply the reported 30% creator share and the 2,885th-best emoticon of
all time returned roughly **₩30M cumulative, pre-tax, before splitting between two people.**

**Design note:** with Emoticon Plus (2.5M+ subscribers) distributing revenue by *usage share*, the
asset that pays is the **everyday reaction sticker**, not the showpiece illustration.

### 🔶 Naver OGQ Market — for the merch pipeline

The only one of the three with a **native physical-goods flow** — creators sell acrylic keyrings,
card stickers, phone cases directly, without a manufacturer relationship. Also auto-registers work
to Korea's UCI content-identifier system, which is a cheap provenance hedge.

⚠️ **Revenue share is not published anywhere.** Do not model it. Settlement runs through a Korean
bank account — assume Korean-resident-oriented until confirmed.

🚩 **Directly relevant to this studio:** OGQ's 2025/26 terms require **AI-use labelling** on
uploads — *no AI / partial AI / AI used*. Mislabelling escalates to **settlement holds and account
termination.** AI use itself is **not** penalised; false or non-transparent labelling is. Given the
studio's [[../legal/ai-made-art-and-copyright|accepted use of AI]], this is a live compliance point.

### ❌ Telegram, WhatsApp, iMessage, Discord

**No confirmed creator revenue-sharing sticker marketplace on any of them** as of August 2026.
Telegram's Fragment handles usernames and Stars, not stickers. Their value is distribution and
habit, not income — and their discovery value is near zero, because none surfaces an artist
identity next to the sticker.

⚠️ Coverage here was incomplete; treat as unverified rather than confirmed-absent.

## Recommended sequence

1. **LINE first.** Open, fast, informative. Learn what lands.
2. **Design for send frequency**, not per-pack sales.
3. **Own the characters first** — see the 잔망루피 warning above.
4. **Kakao later**, once a character has external traction.
5. **OGQ** only if a Korean entity exists, and for the merch pipeline rather than the revenue.
