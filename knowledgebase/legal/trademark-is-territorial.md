# Trademark is territorial

**There is no global trademark.** Each country grants its own. A US registration protects you in
the US and nowhere else.

This is the opposite of [[copyright-is-already-global|copyright]], which you already hold
worldwide for free. Trademark is the one that needs real work.

## Copyright vs trademark — what each actually does

| | Protects | Scope |
|---|---|---|
| **Copyright** | The *work* — the drawing, the story | Global, automatic |
| **Trademark** | The *brand identifier* — the name, the logo as a source marker, character names used to sell things | Country by country, requires registration for full force |

Copyrighting the logo stops someone copying **the drawing**. It does nothing about a competitor
using a *different-looking* mark that still confuses customers about who made something. Closing
that gap is what trademark does.

## ™ vs ® — the practical difference

**™ — use it now. Free. No registration.**
- Asserts an unregistered claim to a mark.
- Requires nothing from anyone. No filing, no fee, no permission.
- Understood internationally.
- Common-law rights can arise from actual use in commerce, limited to where you actually trade.

**® — only after a registration issues, and only in that country.**
- Using ® without a registration is **improper**, and in some countries carries a penalty.
- If you hold a US registration but not a Japanese one, you use ® in the US and ™ in Japan.

```
Midori Fuwafuwa™
```

## What you register

Not one thing — several, each its own filing:

1. **The studio name** — "Midori Fuwafuwa"
2. **The logo** — as a source identifier
3. **Character and product names**, if they're used to sell — "The Kits", "Whimsy"

And each filing names **classes** — the categories of goods and services it covers. Fees are per
class. Likely relevant here:

| Class | Covers |
|---|---|
| 16 | Printed matter, books |
| 9 | Software, apps, downloadable media |
| 41 | Entertainment, publishing services |
| 25 | Clothing *(if merchandise)* |
| 28 | Toys, games *(if merchandise)* |

## The key simplification

**You only need registration where you actually do business.** Not everywhere people can see your
work. Someone browsing from a country where you have no sales and no registration is not a
practical threat.

Register as reach grows — with one exception, which is [[china-first-to-file|China]].
