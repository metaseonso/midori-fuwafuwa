# Privacy policy text

With cookieless analytics and a `mailto:` link, the entire policy is short and **true** — which is
what makes it strong. A long policy full of carve-outs reads as evasive. Four honest paragraphs read
as trustworthy.

## Launch version — mailto only

> **Analytics.** We use Cloudflare Web Analytics to count visits. It sets no cookies, stores no
> identifiers, and does not track you across sites or sessions. We see aggregate numbers only —
> never you.
>
> **Hosting.** Our site is hosted by Cloudflare, which processes your IP address in order to
> deliver the page and to protect the site from abuse. We do not use it to identify you.
>
> **Contact.** If you email us, we have your email — because you sent it. We use it to reply and
> nothing else.
>
> **That is everything.** No accounts, no forms, no advertising, no data sold or shared, ever.

⚠️ **Accuracy note, 2026-08-19.** An earlier draft said simply "we collect nothing." That is not
strictly accurate and should not be claimed: **every website transiently processes visitor IP
addresses** — the host cannot deliver a page without one. Cloudflare may also set strictly-necessary
cookies (`__cf_bm`, `cf_clearance`, `_cfuvid`) depending on which protection features are enabled.

Naming the processor and being precise costs two sentences and is *stronger* than an
overclaim — the whole value of this policy is that every line of it is true. If the analytics tool
changes from Cloudflare, change the name in the text.

## When the AI reception ships

Add one paragraph. See [[contact-without-collecting]] for the architecture this assumes.

> **The reception.** You can talk to our AI receptionist. Your conversation stays in your browser —
> we do not store it, and it is gone when you close the tab. Messages are sent to our AI provider to
> generate a reply and are not retained by them. We keep a count of how many conversations happen,
> and nothing about what was said. If you want a reply by email, you can choose to leave an
> address — that is up to you, and we use it only to answer.

## Notes on the copy

- **"That is everything"** is doing real work. It is a positive claim, not a hedge, and only
  possible because it is accurate. Do not keep the sentence if it stops being true.
- **The training clause** in the Terms is deliberate. It does not stop scraping on its own, but it
  makes the studio's position explicit, which matters if it ever needs to be argued.
- **Keep both pages one screen long.** If either grows, something was added to the site that should
  have been a deliberate decision — check it against [[collect-nothing]].
